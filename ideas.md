# IDEAS

* **Project Description**: A health monitoring gadget special but not limited to livestock.

## TIERS:
- Continous ear tag: this should be the cheapest one for all cattles in the herd, meaning that itakuwa na fewest components. **_PILOT_**
- Diagnostic neck tag: for those flagged anomally, this monitors them closely. Have like extra kind sensors for temperature, microphone and comm modules.

> From the tiers, kuna kuwa na gateway moja kwenye zizi au sehemu wanayotulia usiku ya kuanalyze daily data for each cattle na kutoa alerts.

## TECHNICAL:
* Ear tag: inakuwa na accelerometer na BLE ambayo zitaweza kusurvive kwa coin cell tu kwa mda mrefu hata miaka 2 without battery change. Should be able to store data too for tracking.
